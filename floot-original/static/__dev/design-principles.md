Licença News is an editorial rights exchange: a modern newsroom ledger, not a generic SaaS dashboard. The visual thread is the authority of a well-edited newspaper translated into a precise digital rights instrument: warm paper, ink-dark typography, restrained oxblood red, hairline rules, compact metadata, and tabular financial figures. Light-first with a deliberate dark mode. Dashboard pacing is tight; public marketing areas can breathe. Use Newsreader for display headlines, IBM Plex Sans for interface/body, and IBM Plex Mono for IDs, dates and money. Surfaces feel like layered paper and archival folders: square-ish corners, subtle borders, almost no decorative gradients, and clear status labels. Red is reserved for primary actions and important rights states; never use it as decoration.
